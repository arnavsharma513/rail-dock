FAQ's

What is Rail Dock?

Rail Dock is a website that provides information about Indian Railways, including PNR status, train schedules, and live train status.

How can I check my PNR status?

You can check your PNR status by entering your PNR number in the "PNR Status" section and clicking the "Submit" button.

How can I search for trains?

You can search for trains by entering the train number in the "Search By Train No." section or by entering the source and destination stations in the "Search Train" section.

How can I check the live status of a train?

You can check the live status of a train by entering the train number in the "Live Status" section and clicking the "Submit" button.

Is Rail Dock free to use?

Yes, Rail Dock is completely free to use.

Is Rail Dock affiliated with Indian Railways?

No, Rail Dock is an independent website and is not affiliated with Indian Railways.
